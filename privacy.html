1. Overview

Receipts ("Receipts," "we," "our," or "us") is a football match prediction game created and operated by Devansh Raj Mishra under the Copa brand.

Receipts is designed to allow football fans to predict match results and scorelines, track points, utilize weekly locks, compete on leaderboards, and manage profile preferences (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you.

Contact: hq@getcopa.com

By creating an account, accessing, or using Receipts, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information generated through your use of the Service (such as match predictions, points, and lock selections), and limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve Receipts.

  • Notification preferences
  • Account status information

Receipts is a competitive prediction game. We collect and store:

  • Match score predictions and outcome selections

2.4 Device and Technical Information
