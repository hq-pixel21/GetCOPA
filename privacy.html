Privacy Policy

COPA · Last updated: April 27, 2026

1. Overview

COPA ("COPA," "we," "our," or "us") is an independent football community app created and operated by Devansh Raj Mishra.

COPA is a user-focused digital product built to help football fans connect through communities, match discussions, profiles, posts, comments, reactions, notifications, reporting tools, blocking tools, moderation tools, and related features (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you.

Contact: hq@getcopa.com

By creating an account, accessing, or using COPA, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information generated through your use of the Service, and limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve COPA.

2.1 Account and Profile Information

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Authentication information handled through third-party providers
  • Profile image
  • Bio
  • Favorite clubs, leagues, or communities
  • Notification preferences
  • Account status information

2.2 Public Content and Activity

COPA is social by design. We may collect and store:

  • Posts
  • Replies
  • Comments
  • Quoted posts
  • Images
  • GIFs
  • Links
  • Embedded media
  • Reactions and engagement activity
  • Matchroom discussions
  • Public profile elements associated with your content

Content you choose to make public may be viewed, copied, shared, quoted, or screenshotted by others.

2.3 Safety, Moderation, and Support Information

  • Reports submitted by users
  • Moderation decisions
  • Blocked-user relationships
  • Safety review records
  • Support messages
  • Feedback requests

2.4 Device and Technical Information

  • App version
  • Device type/model
  • Operating system version
  • Push notification token(s)
  • Crash logs
  • Performance diagnostics
  • Security or anti-abuse signals

2.5 Third-Party Features

If you use third-party features such as GIF search, embedded links, sports data, or external media, certain related information may be processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy practices.

  • Review reports and enforce platform rules

  • Improve reliability, features, and performance
  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations
